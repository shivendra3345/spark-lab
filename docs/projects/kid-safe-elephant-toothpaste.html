Safety first

Wear goggles. Use only 3% drugstore hydrogen peroxide and have an adult pour it.

Step by step

Tick each step as you go. Your progress is saved on this device.

What's going on?

Yeast contains an enzyme called catalase that breaks hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen very quickly. The oxygen gets trapped by the soap, making a huge column of foam. The bottle warms because the reaction gives off heat.
