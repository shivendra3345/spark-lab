Safety first

Throw oobleck in the trash, not down the sink. It can clog drains.

Step by step

Tick each step as you go. Your progress is saved on this device.

What's going on?

Oobleck is a non-Newtonian fluid. When you push hard, the cornstarch grains jam together and act like a solid. When you move slowly, they slide past each other and flow like a liquid.

Try this next

Put oobleck on a speaker cone wrapped in plastic and play bass music to see it dance.
