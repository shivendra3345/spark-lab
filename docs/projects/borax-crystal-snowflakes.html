Safety first

Borax is not safe to eat. An adult should mix it, and everyone washes hands afterwards.

Step by step

Tick each step as you go. Your progress is saved on this device.

What's going on?

The hot water holds lots of dissolved borax. As it cools, the water can hold less, so borax crystals grow on the fuzzy pipe cleaner, which gives them lots of places to start.
