Safety first

Step by step

What's going on?

Raw potato contains catalase, an enzyme that breaks hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen bubbles. Cooking destroys the enzyme, so cooked potato barely bubbles.

Try this next

Test apple, liver (with an adult) and carrot to compare enzyme strength.
