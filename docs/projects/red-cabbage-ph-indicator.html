What's going on?

Red cabbage contains a pigment called anthocyanin that changes color depending on how acidic or basic a liquid is. Acids turn it pink and red, bases turn it green or yellow.

Try this next

Soak strips of paper towel in the juice and dry them to make your own pH test strips.
