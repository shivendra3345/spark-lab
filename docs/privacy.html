Privacy policy

What Spark Lab collects, and what it doesn't. Written for grown-ups, in plain words.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask children for names, emails, photos or any other personal information.
  • Your favorites and ticked-off steps are saved only in your own browser, on your own device. We can't see them.
  • There are no comment boxes, chat or sign-up forms on this site.
  • We count page visits anonymously, without cookies, to see which projects people like.

Information stored on your device

When you save a favorite or tick a step, your browser stores that in its local storage so it's still there next time. It never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Services from other companies

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Video buttons open YouTube in a new tab, where YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Visitor statistics

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't build profiles of visitors. It records things like which pages are viewed, the referring website, browser type, screen size and country, so we can see which projects are popular and improve the site.

Children's privacy

Spark Lab is made for children, families and teachers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
