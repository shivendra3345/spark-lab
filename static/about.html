About Spark Lab

A free library of hands-on science projects for kids, families and classrooms.

What you'll find here

Every project has the same parts so it's easy to follow: a materials checklist, numbered steps you can tick off, a plain-language explanation of the science, an idea to take it further, safety notes where needed, and a link to videos of the project being done.

Projects are grouped into twelve topics and labelled with an age range, a difficulty level and how long they take, so you can find the right one in seconds.

About the videos

Each project links to videos of that experiment on YouTube, made by teachers, science museums and science channels. Videos open on YouTube in a new tab. Grown-ups should preview videos before sharing them with children.

Use it in class or at home

Every project page prints as a one-sheet worksheet, with space for predictions and results. Feel free to print and share copies with your students.

A note on safety

The projects use everyday materials, but science still needs care. Read the safety rules before you start, and always have an adult nearby, especially for anything involving heat, sharp tools or chemicals.
