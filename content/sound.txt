# String Telephone
e=📞 a=4-10 d=Easy t=20 min
m: 2 paper or plastic cups; 5-10 m of string; sharp pencil; paper clips
- Poke a small hole in the bottom of each cup.
- Thread the string through each hole and tie a paper clip inside to hold it.
- Walk apart until the string is tight.
- Talk into one cup while a friend listens with the other.
w: Your voice makes the cup bottom vibrate. The tight string carries the vibrations to the other cup, which turns them back into sound.
x: Try fishing line, yarn and wire. Which sounds clearest?

# Echo Measurement
e=🗣️ a=10-14 d=Medium t=30 min
m: large outdoor wall; two wooden blocks; tape measure; stopwatch
- Stand about 50 m from a large wall.
- Clap blocks together and listen for the echo.
- Clap in a rhythm so each clap matches the echo.
- Time 20 claps and work out the time for one.
- Divide the distance there and back (100 m) by that time.
w: Echoes are sound bouncing back. Timing them lets you calculate the speed of sound, about 343 meters per second.
x: Compare your result with the real speed of sound.

# Doppler Swing
e=📲 a=10-14 d=Medium t=15 min
m: phone with tone generator app or small buzzer; sock; string; open space

- Tie the sock to a string.
