# Bean in a Bag
e=🫘 a=4-10 d=Easy t=1-2 weeks
m: dried beans (lima or kidney); zip bag; paper towel; water; tape; window
- Wet a paper towel and fold it into the bag.
- Place 3 beans on the towel where you can see them.
- Seal the bag and tape it to a sunny window.
- Check every day and draw what you see.
- Watch roots grow down and a shoot grow up.
w: Seeds hold a baby plant and a food supply. With water and warmth, the seed germinates, sending roots down and a shoot up.
x: Turn the bag upside down after roots appear. Which way do they grow?

# Celery Color Change
e=🥬 a=4-10 d=Easy t=1 day
m: celery stalks with leaves; clear cups; water; food coloring; knife (adult)
- Fill cups with water and add lots of food coloring.
- An adult trims the bottom of each celery stalk.
- Stand the celery in the cups.
- Check after a few hours and the next day.
- Cut a stalk across to see the colored tubes.
w: Plants have tiny tubes called xylem that carry water from the roots to the leaves. The colored water shows the path.
x: Split one stalk in two and put each half in a different color.

# Color-Changing Flowers
e=🌸 a=4-11 d=Easy t=1-2 days
m: white carnations or daisies; vases or cups; water; food coloring
- Fill cups with water and color.
- Trim flower stems at an angle.
- Place one flower in each cup.
- Watch the petals change color over 1 to 2 days.
w: Water travels up the stem and evaporates from the petals. The color is left behind in the petals.
x: Split a stem lengthwise and put each half in a different color for a two-tone flower.

# Shoebox Plant Maze
e=🌱 a=6-12 d=Medium t=2-3 weeks
m: shoebox with lid; cardboard pieces; tape; small potted bean plant; scissors
- Cut a hole in one end of the shoebox.
- Tape cardboard walls inside to make a maze.
- Put the plant at the opposite end from the hole.
- Close the lid and put the box near a sunny window.
- Water every few days and watch it find the light.
w: Plants grow toward light. This is called phototropism. A plant hormone makes the shaded side of the stem grow faster.
x: Make a more complex maze with more turns.

# Leaf Chromatography
e=🍂 a=8-13 d=Medium t=1 hour
m: green leaves; rubbing alcohol (adult); jars; coffee filter strips; plastic wrap; bowl of hot water
- Tear leaves into tiny pieces and put them in a jar.
- An adult covers them with rubbing alcohol.
- Cover with plastic wrap and set the jar in hot water for 30 minutes.
- Put a coffee filter strip into the green liquid.
- Watch colors separate as they rise.
w: Leaves contain several pigments. Green chlorophyll hides yellow and orange pigments that show up in fall.
x: Test leaves from different trees.
s: Adults handle rubbing alcohol. Keep it away from heat and flames.

# Do Plants Need Light?

e=🔄 a=8-12 d=Medium t=1 week

# Measure a Tree's Height
e=🌳 a=8-13 d=Easy t=20 min
m: stick the same length as your arm; tape measure; tree
- Hold the stick straight up at arm's length.
- Walk back until the stick covers the whole tree.
- Mark where you stand.
- Measure from you to the tree. That's about the height.
w: The stick and your arm make a right triangle with equal sides, so the distance equals the tree's height.
x: Compare different trees in your area.
