# Growing Bacteria in Petri Dishes
e=🦠 a=9-14 d=Medium t=3-5 days
m: pre-made agar petri dishes; cotton swabs; tape; marker; warm dark place
- Label each dish with what you will test (doorknob, phone, hands, keyboard).
- Rub a swab on each surface, then gently zigzag it across the agar.
- Tape the lids closed and store the dishes upside down somewhere warm.
- Check every day and draw what grows.
- When done, an adult throws the sealed dishes away without opening them.
w: Bacteria and mold are everywhere but too small to see. On agar, each microbe multiplies into millions, forming a colony big enough to see.
x: Swab a hand before and after washing to see how well soap works.
s: Never open dishes after growth. Seal them in a bag and throw them away.

# Strawberry DNA Extraction
e=🍓 a=8-14 d=Medium t=30 min
m: 3 strawberries; zip bag; 2 tsp dish soap; 1/2 tsp salt; 1/2 cup water; coffee filter; clear cup; ice-cold rubbing alcohol (adult); wooden skewer
- Mash the strawberries in the bag for 2 minutes.
- Mix water, soap and salt, add 2 spoons to the bag and mash gently for 1 more minute.
- Pour through a coffee filter into a clear cup.
- An adult slowly pours cold rubbing alcohol down the side to make a layer on top.
- Watch white strands rise. Twirl them out with a skewer.
w: Soap breaks open the cells, and salt helps DNA clump together. DNA does not dissolve in alcohol, so it floats up as white strands you can see.
x: Try bananas, kiwis and spinach.
s: Rubbing alcohol is flammable and poisonous. Adults only.

# Yeast Balloon Fermentation
e=🎈 a=7-12 d=Easy t=45 min
m: packet of dry yeast; warm water; sugar; plastic bottle; balloon
- Pour 1 cup warm water into the bottle.
- Add the yeast and 2 tsp sugar and swirl.
- Stretch the balloon over the top.
- Wait 20 to 40 minutes and watch it inflate.
w: Yeast is a living fungus. It eats sugar and gives off carbon dioxide gas, which fills the balloon. This is why bread rises.
x: Try no sugar, cold water and hot water. Which works best?

- Make one dry, one damp and one touched by unwashed hands.

# Ant Observation Farm
e=🐜 a=6-12 d=Medium t=2 weeks
m: large clear jar; smaller jar inside; sandy soil; cotton ball; honey; crumbs; black paper; cloth and rubber band
- Put the small jar inside the big one so ants tunnel near the glass.
- Fill the gap with loose damp soil.
- Gently collect a few ants from one nest outdoors.
- Cover with cloth, add a damp cotton ball and a drop of honey.
- Wrap black paper around the jar and lift it daily to see the tunnels.
w: Ants work as a team, building tunnels and sharing food. Darkness encourages them to dig near the glass.
x: Draw a map of the tunnels each day.
s: Avoid fire ants and biting ants. Release the ants where you found them after two weeks.
